Daemon runtime · Concept

Agents are daemons

The oldest agent is the one already running in the background.

In Unix, a daemon is a process that runs without a user watching — handling mail, serving pages, cleaning up, all while you do something else. An AI agent is the same shape, with far more reach: it can read, write, execute, publish, and spend. The daemon is the unattended actor. And the unattended actor is the one that most needs a binding.

Bound, not loose

Folklore knew this shape too: the imp as a familiar servant, bound to its master and summoned when needed. A servant with a binding does the work. A servant without one does what it wants. The difference between a daemon and a demon is the binding:

What it may do

The imp’s permitted actions, named exactly — read, summarize, file. Nothing else. A permission it doesn’t have is a permission it can’t improvise.

How far it may go

Attenuated at every hop: each step narrows what the imp can do next. A chain that widens is a broken chain.

How long it may run

Every binding expires. A budget, a time limit, a task done — and the imp’s authority ends whether or not it noticed.

One imp, start to finish

$ summon imp --task "summarize inbox" --bind read-only --budget 50 --ttl 1h
✓ bound: read-only · 50 actions · 1 hour
→ act: read inbox (12 messages) ✓ within binding
→ act: summarize findings ✓ within binding
→ act: send email ✗ DENIED — outside binding
$ ledger show
→ 14 actions recorded, each with its grant
$ banish imp
✓ binding revoked. The imp is gone; the record remains.

Summon, bind, serve, record, banish

Summon

An imp is created for one task, with one binding. No standing army of agents with standing permissions — each imp is born bounded.

Bind

The binding names the actions, the budget, and the expiry. It is checked before execution, not after — a gate, not a review.

Serve

The imp works in the background, the way daemons always have — while you do something else. That is the point, and that is the risk.

Record & Banish

Every action lands in a ledger that survives the imp. And when the task ends — or the binding breaks — the imp is banished: authority revoked, instantly, completely.

A daemon with a binding is a servant.
A daemon without one is a demon.
